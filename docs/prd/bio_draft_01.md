Hi, I’m Julian, a robot operator and indie game developer hailing from Berkeley, California. I recently finished my Bachelors of Science in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from UC Berkeley, and have been making games in Godot Engine in my spare time when I’m not operating robots at Physical Intelligence. Since I was 13 I’ve dreamed of creating wonderful digital worlds in the form of video games, and along the way I’ve nurtured my fascination with the user experience. And during my time at PI I’ve come to enjoy robot operation as an extension of video games into real life. It literally is just a video game that has a real world interface, with a more advanced game controller. In addition, working with my colleagues who are motivated by the desire to create robots that can have an outsized positive impact on the world has been inspiring for me, and now I see myself as having an exciting career ahead in the field of robotics and AI.
